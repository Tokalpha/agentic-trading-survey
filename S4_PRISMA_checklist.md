# PRISMA 2020 reporting checklist and limitations

Survey v2.2. This checklist follows the manuscript appendix “PRISMA 2020 Reporting Checklist and Limitations.” Historical retrieval completeness and independent human coding reliability remain unresolved. The review was not prospectively registered and does not pool trading-performance effect sizes.

| Item | Reporting item | Status and manuscript location |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title: identify the report as a systematic review | The title identifies an architecture, pipeline and evaluation survey; incomplete historical search provenance and the targeted update limit systematic-review claims. |
| 2 | Abstract: structured summary | The unstructured abstract summarizes the aim, corpus, descriptive findings, synthesis, and proposed design. |
| 3 | Rationale | Introduction; “Axis 1: The Six-Stage Trading Pipeline” to “Axis 2: Six Cognitive Architecture Families”. |
| 4 | Objectives: research questions | Introduction: research questions on workflow functions and evidence for architectural choices. |
| 5 | Eligibility criteria | “Search, Eligibility, and Evidence Accounting”: eligibility, transferability and role-assignment criteria. |
| 6 | Information sources: databases and dates of last search | “Search, Eligibility, and Evidence Accounting”: historical five-provider record and a logged targeted update through October 6, 2026; access failures retained. Historical retrieval completeness is unresolved. |
| 7 | Search strategy: full query strings | Historical keyword families and exact update queries/interfaces are retained in the authors’ private search log; a complete original per-provider query export is unavailable. Partially addressed. |
| 8 | Selection process: reviewers, automation, conflict resolution | The authors screened studies, made eligibility decisions, and verified preliminary LLM-assisted extraction against source documents. See Author Contributions for individual roles and the methodology section and Acknowledgment for the tools and their roles. The authors recorded unresolved eligibility in their private screening ledger. No independent human inter-rater agreement is reported. Partially addressed. |
| 9 | Data collection process | Versioned raw workbook plus update overlays; 191 methodology and 33 benchmark/comparator records; pinned source locators for update claims. |
| 10a | Data items: outcomes sought | Six stage-coverage indicators and six architecture-family indicators. |
| 10b | Data items: other variables | Code availability, prompt transparency, dataset accessibility. |
| 11 | Study risk-of-bias assessment | No formal risk-of-bias scoring; reproducibility assessed narratively in “Axis 1: The Six-Stage Trading Pipeline” and Appendix M, “High-Impact Research Opportunities”. Partially addressed. |
| 12 | Effect measures | Descriptive coverage proportions and assignment ratios are reported; no trading-performance effect sizes are pooled. |
| 13a | Synthesis: eligibility for each synthesis | Methodology records define the architecture/stage denominator; benchmark/comparator and contextual records are separately identified. |
| 13b | Synthesis: data preparation | The authors converted seven annotated stage fields into the six stages used in the paper by combining the original second and third fields. |
| 13c | Synthesis: tabulation and visualization | Dual-axis heatmap (Figure 3); Axis 2 and Axis 1 distribution figures. |
| 13d | Synthesis: methods to synthesize results | Narrative synthesis by stage and by architecture; no quantitative pooling. |
| 13e | Synthesis: methods to explore heterogeneity | Cross-axis interaction analysis in “Dual-Axis Distribution and Cross-Axis Findings”. |
| 13f | Synthesis: sensitivity analyses | The authors used the underlying annotations to compare paper and assignment counts, measure the influence of removing one paper, and test stricter coding and single-family subsets. |
| 14 | Reporting-bias assessment | English-language scholarly indexes and English-only queries may bias the corpus toward English, publicly indexed work; non-English and proprietary industry reports are likely under-represented. |
| 15 | Certainty assessment | Not applicable (no GRADE-style certainty rating). |
| 16a | Study selection: numbers identified/screened/included/excluded | Figure 6 reports 224 verified included records (191 methodology and 33 benchmark/comparator). The historical identification, deduplication, screening, and exclusion counts cannot be fully reconstructed. Partially addressed. |
| 16b | Reasons for exclusion | Record-level update dispositions and reopened legacy exclusions are in the authors’ private screening ledger; inaccessible primary text remains unresolved. |
| 17 | Study characteristics: per-study citation/characteristics | The [methodology table](S1_methodology_corpus.csv) and [benchmark/comparator table](S2_benchmark_corpus.csv) identify studies and report architecture labels and six-stage indicators. The authors retain the coding reasons and source locations privately. |
| 18 | Risk of bias in studies | As item 11: narrative only. Partially addressed. |
| 19 | Results of individual studies | “Axis 2: Six Cognitive Architecture Families” and “Axis 1: The Six-Stage Trading Pipeline” report stage/architecture findings per cluster. |
| 20a | Results of syntheses: characteristics and risk-of-bias | “Axis 2: Six Cognitive Architecture Families”, “Axis 1: The Six-Stage Trading Pipeline”, and Appendix G, “Architecture Conflicts and Consensus”: cluster-level discussion. |
| 20b | Results of syntheses: quantitative summary | “Research-Deployment Gap and Corpus Trends”: 2.63 assignment ratio and 48.7% without defense engagement, with explicit denominators. |
| 20c | Results of syntheses: heterogeneity investigations | “Dual-Axis Distribution and Cross-Axis Findings” (cross-axis interaction findings). |
| 20d | Results of syntheses: sensitivity analyses | The authors compared paper-level engagement with counts of stage assignments. The detailed sensitivity output is retained privately. |
| 21 | Reporting biases | As item 14. |
| 22 | Certainty of evidence | No formal certainty assessment is available; evidence limitations are stated per synthesis and source. |
| 23a | Discussion: interpretation of results | Appendix M, “High-Impact Research Opportunities” (prioritized research roadmap). |
| 23b | Discussion: limitations of the evidence | “Evaluation Methodology and Benchmarks” and “Search, Eligibility, and Evidence Accounting”: evaluation heterogeneity, version/access limits and incomplete operational evidence. |
