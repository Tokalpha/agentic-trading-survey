# LLM-Powered Agentic Trading

Reader tables for Survey v2.2, *LLM-Powered Agentic Trading: A Survey of Architecture Mechanisms, Pipeline Functions and Evaluation Evidence*.

**Paper and appendices.** The official paper link will be available here soon.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Methodology corpus](S1_methodology_corpus.csv) | 191 studies used to calculate stage and architecture coverage |
| [Benchmark and comparator corpus](S2_benchmark_corpus.csv) | 33 records classified separately |
| [Temporal cohort summaries](S3_temporal_trends.csv) | Counts, coverage and date boundaries for six cohorts |
| [Temporal assignments](S3_temporal_paper_assignments.csv) | Cohort and date for each methodology study |
| [PRISMA 2020 checklist](S4_PRISMA_checklist.md) | Where the manuscript reports its review methods, results and limitations |

**Reading the tables.** Use the citation key to match each methodology study to its temporal assignment. A study can have several architecture labels and engage several stages. Architecture membership counts treat Yes and Partial as positive annotations, regardless of capitalization. A stage value of 1 means the coding recorded some engagement, including partial or minimal engagement. A zero means there was no positive annotation. The binary stage columns do not distinguish full, partial and minimal engagement. For two comparator records with missing dates and architecture annotations, the zeros do not establish absence. Dates can refer to submission or publication. The final cohort ends on October 6, 2026.
